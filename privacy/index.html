Tiger Lugs Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026
Operated by: VinDee LLC ("we", "us")
Contact: support@tigerlugs.com

Tiger Lugs is an app for keeping and enjoying your watch collection. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Tiger Lugs apps for iPhone and Android, the Tiger Lugs web app, and the tigerlugs.com website.

1. What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Apple, Google, or an emailed code, we receive your email address and create an account ID. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive the relay address Apple provides, not your real email.

Your collection. Everything you add to Tiger Lugs is stored on your device and, once you're signed in, synced to our servers so it's available on your other devices:

  • Watches (brand, model, reference, specifications, purchase details and prices you enter, warranty and service details, notes), including watches on your wishlist
  • Wear logs, Naked Wrist Days, and notes you add to them
  • Watch boxes and where each watch sits in them
  • Photos you add of your watches
  • App settings such as reminder times and which tips you've dismissed

We also keep the last 20 earlier versions of your collection, so you can restore it if something goes wrong ("Cloud History").

Usage analytics. We use PostHog to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are visited, whether adding a watch was completed, and which features are used. These events are tied to a random identifier for your device, not to your account or email. They can include the brand of a watch you select while adding one. They never include your photos, prices, or notes.

Streak leaderboard (only if you join). Our servers record when each wear log first reaches them; this is how the leaderboard counts only days logged on the day itself. If you choose to join the leaderboard, we also store a randomly generated handle (for example "Silent Tourbillon") and your time zone. Other users can see your handle and your streak length. They never see your name, email, or collection.

Launch list (tigerlugs.com). If you join the launch list on our website, we collect your email address, the kind of phone you use if you tell us (iPhone or Android), and how you found us (for example, a link from Reddit). We use it only to tell you when Tiger Lugs launches and about early access. Every email includes an unsubscribe link; unsubscribe or email us and we'll remove you from the list. The website doesn't use cookies or analytics.

What we don't collect. We don't collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. The Time Grapher tool uses your microphone to listen to your watch's ticking. That audio is processed on your device and is never recorded, stored, or sent to us.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: store and sync your collection, sign you in, run the streak leaderboard, and send the wear reminders you've turned on (scheduled on your device).
  • To fill in watch details: when a watch isn't in our built-in catalog, the brand, model, and reference you typed are sent to an AI service to suggest its specifications.
  • To prepare your photos: photos you add are sent to an image service that removes the background so the watch sits cleanly on its cushion.
  • To improve the app, using the anonymous analytics described above.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. How long we keep it

Your collection is kept for as long as you have an account. Cloud History keeps your 20 most recent earlier versions. Items you delete are removed from our servers on the next sync. If you delete your account, everything is kept for 30 days so you can restore it, then permanently deleted. When you sign out, the data on that device is cleared. Analytics events are kept for a limited period under our analytics provider's retention settings and then deleted.

5. Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings › Delete Account. Your account is removed from the streak leaderboard straight away and permanently deleted, with all synced data, 30 days later. Until then you can restore it by signing in again. You can also email support@tigerlugs.com from the address on your account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; manage that in your store account settings.
  • Leave the leaderboard at any time. Your handle and leaderboard record are deleted.
  • Notifications can be turned off in Settings or in your device settings.
  • Access or correct your data in the app, or by contacting us.
  • Do Not Track. The app doesn't track you across sites, so it doesn't respond differently to Do Not Track signals.

6. Children

Tiger Lugs is for people 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account's data is protected by access rules so it can only be read by that account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Where we operate

Tiger Lugs is currently offered in the United States, and data is processed in the United States.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. For material changes we'll tell you in the app.
