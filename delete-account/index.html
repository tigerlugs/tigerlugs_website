How to delete your account

In the app: open Settings, tap Delete Account under Sign Out, and follow the steps. You'll be signed out and we'll email you the date your account will be permanently deleted.

By email: if you can't open the app, email support@tigerlugs.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account".

30 days to change your mind

Your account is permanently deleted 30 days after you ask. Until then, sign in to Tiger Lugs and tap Restore my account to get everything back. After 30 days it can't be undone.

What's deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Every watch, wishlist item, photo, wear log, Naked Wrist Day, watch box and note synced to our servers
  • Your Cloud History (earlier versions of your collection)
  • Your streak leaderboard handle and record, if you joined (removed straight away)
  • Your launch-list signup, if you joined it

Anonymous usage analytics aren't linked to your account, so they can't be traced back to you. They expire under our analytics provider's retention settings.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings so you aren't charged again.
